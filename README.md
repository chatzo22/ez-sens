# EZ Sens
Original aim trainer + sensitivity converter/finder. Live: https://chatzo22.github.io/ez-sens/

- `index.html` is the whole app (single file, works offline; three.js r160 MIT is bundled inline — see `vendor/THREE-LICENSE.txt`).
- Source parts: `src.head.html`, `src.math.js`, `src.app.js` → `python3 build.py` builds `index.html`.
- Tests: `node test/math.test.js` (conversion math vs. published reference points, plus Vandal / AK-47 damage tables). Headless UI: `node test/app.headless.js`, `node test/range.headless.js`, `node test/env.headless.js` (Finder Retro Horizon/Sunrise/Day/Night), `node test/flash.headless.js` (Flash dodge, Light theme, situational maps, peek bots).
- 16 built-in games + custom games (enter a yaw or calibrate). Yaw values and sources are listed in the app's About tab.
- Tracking targets bounce off the floor and ceiling so they never go underground. An optional off-screen hint (on by default) paints a red-to-magenta glow on the screen edge that follows a target you can't see, in Train, Finder and the Range.
- Train uses a compact mid-run HUD and an end-of-round recap (score, accuracy, time-to-hit, first-shot time, local best). Hits and misses flash a short ring on the crosshair.
- Each game has its own crosshair (style, color, length, thickness, gap, outline, center dot, opacity, optional dynamic shot spread). Train and Range share it. Export/import an EZX1 code, copy from another game, or paste a classic CSGO- share code.
- The Sensitivity Finder, Train and Flash share arena environments: Retro Horizon (the default — a dark synthwave sky, glowing pink/magenta horizon and neon grid floor, with neon edge trim on Flash maps and a light film-grain overlay), plus Sunrise, Day and Night (Night is the original dark look; Sunrise and Day still show a sun disc). The Range keeps its own look unless you pick Retro Horizon in Range settings. The choice is saved in the browser.
- Dark (default) and Light UI themes, saved in the browser. They change the chrome, not the 3D arenas.
- Flash dodge tab: situational blockout maps inspired by common CS2 and Valorant flash habits (not copies of those maps) — hallway, choke hold, site anchor, site entry, off-angle retake, over-wall pop, mid-window cross, ramp walk, or a random mix. Valorant-style short pop or CS2-style thrown bounce off the geometry, with line-of-sight occlusion. After each pop, original capsule-and-sphere bots start hidden tight to a corner and begin peeking or swinging out 0–150 ms later (staggered), a bit slower than before so they are easier to track (Easy slowest, Hard close to the old speed, plus a 0.5×–1.5× bot-speed slider). Peek style Mix (default) gives each group a mix of slow shoulder peeks, medium-speed wide swings, and a crouched “donk slide” across the opening; Peeks only, Wide swings only and Slides only are also available. If you dodged or were covered, they hold the peek in the open (jiggle or re-swing, and sometimes a mid-window retreat and re-peek) long enough to turn back and shoot — about 1.2–2.5 s depending on difficulty and how far you turned (Easy longest, Hard shortest), then retreat behind cover. If you got flashed, they are already out as the white-out fades and stay peeking for the normal recover window. The off-screen hint points at swinging bots after a dodge (on by default; the existing toggle still applies). Some take wide, fast swing peeks or shoulder-peek; Mix stance — default — is about 40% standing, 30% crouched and 30% switching mid-push, with mixed groups always including both a standing bot and a crouched or switching one; Standing only, Crouch only and Switching only are also available, and the same option applies to Train and Range bot targets). Hitboxes follow stance changes. A head stays visible from the player path; a blind makes them harder to see. Shots raycast against map geometry, so bots behind walls cannot be hit (Train and Range use the same check). Maps have readable wall planes (dark purple or navy in Retro Horizon, with a faint panel texture), neon edge trim on corners, baseboards, door and window frames, peek-corner highlights, and crates with neon edges — still low-poly, no shadows. Recap tracks dodge %, bots killed, peek / wide-swing / slide kills, moving kills, standing vs. crouch kills, time to first hit, turn-back time (pop to first hit after a dodge), accuracy, headshot %, shots-to-kill, TTK and average TTK on pushing bots, and mixed runs are titled Mix. A short Dodged / Partial / Flashed / Covered toast appears after each pop. A one-time photosensitivity notice is a fixed full-screen dialog, a reduced-intensity option is available, flashes never overlap, flash count can be 1–15, Random throws & timing (on by default) varies the side and pop zone (left, right, front, above, behind or off-angle where the map allows), jitters the pop inside that zone, and varies the gap, origin, arc and fuse, while keeping pops out of solids and fair to dodge — the same side never repeats more than twice in a row (turn Random off to practice authored variants). Peek bots usually swing from the side the flash came from, and occasionally from a different corner, and a tinnitus-style ring can be muted or turned down.
- Optional bot targets in Train and Range: a simple original capsule body + sphere head (white body and cyan head by default, with a color setting). Damage models: headshots only; Valorant Vandal with or without heavy shields; CS2 AK-47 with or without kevlar+helmet. Bots have 100 HP plus armor and die at 0 HP. Close-range published damage values are listed in the About tab.

## Windows desktop app
`app/` is an Electron shell that loads the built `index.html` fully offline (custom `ezsens://` scheme, all network blocked).
- `cd app && npm install && npm start` to run locally; `npm run dist` builds `EZ-Sens-Setup-x.y.z.exe` (NSIS) and `EZ-Sens-Portable-x.y.z.exe`.
- Pushing a `v*` tag runs `.github/workflows/desktop.yml` on windows-latest and publishes a GitHub Release.
- View → Unlock FPS (saved, applied next launch) adds `disable-frame-rate-limit` + `disable-gpu-vsync`; the in-app frame cap still applies.
